Learning Tips

🎲 The Die Sides Analogy
A perplexity of 10 means the model was as uncertain as choosing equally among 10 possible words at each step. A perplexity of 1 means 100% certainty.
🔤 Why Prepend Token 50256?
Causal language models predict the next token based on previous tokens. For the first word of a sentence, token 50256 provides the initial conditioning context.
⚖️ Perplexity vs Factuality
Grammatically fluent falsehoods can have very low perplexity if the phrasing is common, while rare factual names can have high perplexity.

Understanding Language Model Perplexity

What is Perplexity?

Perplexity is the standard evaluation metric for probabilistic language models. It is mathematically defined as the exponential of the cross-entropy loss (negative log-likelihood):

Perplexity(W) = exp( - (1 / N) * ∑ ln P(wᵢ | w₁ ... wᵢ₋₁) )

Intuitively, perplexity represents the effective branching factor of the model. If a model has a perplexity of 50 on a test corpus, it was as confused when making predictions as if it had to choose uniformly at random between 50 words at each step.

Why Prepend Token 50256 (<|endoftext|>)?

In causal autoregressive models like GPT-2 and DistilGPT-2, every token prediction is conditioned on prior context: P(tᵢ | t₁ ... tᵢ₋₁). For the very first word in an isolated sentence, there is no previous word. DistilGPT-2 uses token 50256 as the Start-of-Sequence / BOS context marker, allowing the model to compute a well-defined probability for the first sentence token.

Numerically Stable Softmax

Raw model logits can be large positive or negative floating-point numbers. Standard exponentiation exp(z) can easily cause arithmetic overflow in JavaScript. Perplexity Explorer computes stable log-softmax:

ln P(tᵢ) = (z[tᵢ] - max(z)) - ln( ∑ exp(zⱼ - max(z)) )

Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) Subwords

Modern LLMs do not operate on whole words or single characters. They use subword tokenization (BPE). Common words like "love" form a single token, while rare or compound words are segmented into multiple subword pieces.

Important Caveat

Perplexity is an evaluation of statistical likelihood under the model's training distribution. A low perplexity does not imply truth, logical reasoning, or moral alignment.
